Why archive lighting design?

Open access

Every record is free to read, download and cite — no account, no paywall.

Permanent & citable

Files are preserved by Humanities Commons and given a DOI, so they stay findable for the long term.

By designers, for designers

A growing resource for students, researchers, historians and working artists to see how designs were made.

The archive is a project of the Lighting Department at RADA. Have work to share? Find out how to contribute.
